# Crawde 0.1.14

Little walks now move only the crab. Session cards and the native window stay
anchored while it wanders left and right around its placed position.

While Claude is working, walks have 4–8 second pauses, compared with 35–60 seconds
when idle. Movement stays within 100 pixels of home and within the window edges.
Hovering, dragging, keyboard focus, sleep, and reduced motion pause walking.
Pinned chat cards can stay open while the crab wanders.
